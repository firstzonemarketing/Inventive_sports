CHAPTER 04

FAILURE AND RESILIENCE

挫折と再起 • The storm that builds the warrior

The path was neither straight nor gentle.

There were rainy nights when his muscles burned, matches lost at the final second, and moments when exhaustion whispered surrender.

He fell into the mud again and again, his hands blistered, his knees bruised.

Every fall tested his courage. Every mistake taught him something new. Akio learned that progress was not about never falling. It was about choosing to rise.

"The ground is only the foundation from which I will stand taller."

— Akio
